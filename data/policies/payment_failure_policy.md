# Payment Failure Policy

- Policy ID: POL-BILL-014
- Policy name: Payment Failure Resolution and Retry Guidance
- Category: Billing and Collections
- Version: 3.1
- Effective date: 2026-02-10
- Department: Billing Support and Customer Care
- Status: Approved
- Source: Internal Policy Library / Finance Operations

## Purpose
This policy ensures that customer-support teams handle failed payments consistently, reduce avoidable account disruption, and provide clear guidance to customers about next steps. The policy is designed to protect customer trust, reduce involuntary service interruptions, and support the correct payment retry or account review workflow.

## Scope
This policy applies to failed card, bank transfer, direct debit, and digital wallet transactions for customer accounts, recurring subscriptions, and service invoices. It covers support interactions where the customer reports a payment rejection, pending authorization, insufficient funds, expired payment method, or duplicate transaction concern.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the failed transaction details, including amount, date, payment method, and the customer’s account or invoice reference.
2. Determine whether the failure is due to a bank decline, card rejection, expired payment method, authorization issue, or system-side payment gateway error.
3. Review the account to confirm whether the customer is in a retry window, a grace period, or a service suspension state.
4. Ask the customer whether they are using a newly issued card, a card that recently expired, or a bank account with changed limits or verification requirements.
5. If the payment method is still valid, guide the customer through updating the billing details with a secure verification step. If necessary, document the exact date and time that the new method was entered and confirm the transaction status.
6. For retryable failures caused by temporary bank or gateway issues, advise the customer to retry the payment once after the stated waiting period, while ensuring that the case notes reflect the reason for the retry.
7. If the account is at risk of suspension, inform the customer of the impact and provide the next deadline for payment or manual review.
8. Record the root cause, customer communication, action taken, and any follow-up date in the case record. If a transaction is successfully retried or reprocessed, confirm the updated status to the customer and document the payment reference number.

## Exceptions
Exceptions may be approved when:
- a customer disputes a charge and the billing team confirms a potential duplicate, failed authorization, or incorrect amount;
- a payment is blocked by a banking regulation, country-specific requirement, or card network issue outside the customer’s control;
- a manually approved courtesy payment is needed due to an outage or a documented billing system error.

Any exception must be reviewed by the Billing Operations Manager or delegated finance approver before the case is closed. All manual adjustments must be linked to a valid approval reference in the case record.

## Escalation guidance
Escalate to Finance Operations when:
- the payment was taken but the customer’s account reflects an unpaid invoice or duplicate charge;
- the customer disputes the denomination or amount of the charge;
- the payment provider reports a gateway error, batch hold, or unexplained authorization failure;
- multiple retries on the same payment method fail in a short time window.

Escalate to the Support Team Lead when the customer is at risk of immediate service interruption and the payment cannot be resolved through standard support actions. Include the payment identifier, account number, payment method, and the exact steps already taken so the next team can continue without re-verifying the same information.
